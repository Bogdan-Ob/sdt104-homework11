Comprehensive Guide to Our Learning Ecosystem

Everything you need to know about starting your career in the tech industry.

Our Learning Methodology

We believe in a "Practice First" approach. Unlike traditional academic environments, our courses are built around the 70/20/10 model: 70% of learning comes from real-world projects, 20% from peer interaction and mentoring, and only 10% from formal theory.

Project-Based Learning

Every week, students tackle tasks that mirror the daily challenges of professional developers and designers. By the end of the program, you will have a professional portfolio ready for top-tier IT companies.

Career Center & Placement

Education is just the first step. Our dedicated Career Center works with each student to ensure a smooth transition into the workforce. We provide resume workshops, mock interviews, and direct connections to our network of partner companies like Apple, Microsoft, and Samsung.

  • Personal HR-consultant
  • LinkedIn profile optimization
  • Exclusive job openings for graduates

Flexible Learning Formats

We understand that many of our students work or study full-time. That is why we offer both synchronous (live) and asynchronous (recorded) sessions. Our platform is accessible 24/7, allowing you to learn at your own pace from anywhere in the world.
